## 2. Jury grid (rules Art. 6.2, "indicative")

| Criterion | Weight | Organisers' description |
|---|---|---|
| Player experience and game being fun | **25%** | Entertainment value and player enjoyment, reflecting real market expectations for games |
| Technical complexity and execution | **20%** | Functionality, stability, performance, code quality, execution of core gameplay features |
| Originality of content | **20%** | Generative AI is allowed, but original thinking and creativity by the team is what is rewarded |
| Quality of pitch | **15%** | Clarity, structure and impact of the final pitch |
| Adherence to the theme | **10%** | Relevance to the "Green" and recycling themes |
| Mystery challenge | **10%** | An extra challenge announced on the day; teams that build it into the game are rewarded |

Jury: tech professionals, academics, partner representatives. Publicly known so far: Mikael
Ahlström, founder of The Park (Stockholm games and innovation community). The grid is written in
English. Jury decisions are final.

## 3. Rules that constrain how we work

| Rule | What it says | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 8 | Only original projects, **conceived during the hackathon**. Any pre-existing code, design or content **must be explicitly declared to the jury** | We cannot arrive with a finished game. Whatever we bring gets declared |
| Art. 5 | Libraries, images, data and other content must be free, open source or correctly licensed | Keep a `CREDITS.md` from the first asset |
| Art. 12 | Plagiarism, fraud or cheating means disqualification | No reference-game names, layouts, art or decompiled code, ever |
| Art. 4.3 | Every team member must be present for the mandatory phases, the final pitch included | All five on site at 15:30 Sunday |
| Art. 8 | Respect the organisers' deadlines and technical instructions (deposit, pitch format) | Deliverables are deposited at 14:30 Sunday, not at pitch time |
